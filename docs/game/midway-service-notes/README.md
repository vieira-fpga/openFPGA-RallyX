# Midway service bulletins

Notes on two bound compilations of Midway Mfg. Co. service bulletins, filed as
`midwaysvcnotes1.pdf` and `midwaysvcnotes2.pdf`.

These are not Rally-X documents. They are Midway's service department writing to
operators about its whole catalogue, roughly forty games between 1975 and 1982,
and Rally-X is one entry among many. Each volume's table of contents indexes
exactly one bulletin under Rally-X's name, and four catalogue-wide tables name it
in passing. The rest of what has been read is about other machines.

## The two volumes

Neither volume carries a title, a date, an edition number or a compiler's name.
Each simply opens with a `TABLE OF CONTENTS` and runs to a glossary.

|               | notes1                  | notes2                    |
| ------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------- |
| PDF pages     | 92                      | 86                        |
| Printed pages | 1–91                    | 1–87, less two            |
| Rally-X entry | `RALLY-X CODE`, 43–44   | `RALLY X RAM CODE`, 12–13 |
| Last entry    | `GLOSSARY OF TERMS`, 91 | `GLOSSARY OF TERMS`, 87   |

They are overlapping selections, not a two-part set. Both carry
`SELF TESTING RAMS & ROMS` on printed page 1 and `GAME NUMBERS` on printed pages
3–6, both cover Gorf, Galaxian, Pac-Man, Space Invaders and the monitor material,
and both close with a glossary. Neither is a superset of the other. notes1 has
the Tron, `KICK/KICKMAN`, Galaga and Omega Race bulletins that notes2 lacks.
notes2 has Extra Bases, Submarine, 18 Wheeler, Rotation VIII and Sea Wolf II
material that notes1 lacks.

## notes1 is the later compilation

Shared pages are not always the same printing. Printed page 1 is the clearest
case. Both volumes carry a bulletin titled `Roms and Rams Self Testing`, but they
are different revisions of it.

|                    | notes2's page 1                        | notes1's page 1                                  |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Last games listed  | Space Zap, Gorf, Wizard of Wor         | …Kickman, Ms Pac-Man, Roby Roto, Tron, Solar Fox |
| Rally-X row        | grouped, `Galaxian, Pac-Man & Rally X` | its own row, `RALLY X`                           |
| Games with no test | `Invaders & Blue Shark`                | `Invaders, Blue Shark & Lazarian`                |
| Letterhead         | Grand Avenue                           | none                                             |
| Setting            | mixed case                             | full caps                                        |

The newest game on notes2's page is Wizard of Wor. On notes1's it is Solar Fox,
and Lazarian has been added to the no-test list. Both are 1982 titles by the
`GAME NUMBERS` table, against 1981 for Wizard of Wor.

So notes1's copy of that bulletin is the later one, and by extension notes1 is
the later compilation. Nothing in either volume dates itself. That conclusion is
inferred from the contents of one shared page, and no other shared page has been
compared between the volumes.

## Two letterheads

Bulletins inside the volumes appear on two different Midway letterheads, and the
same bulletin can appear on either depending on which volume you look in:

| Letterhead                          | Address                                                | Telephone                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| `MIDWAY MFG. CO. / A BALLY COMPANY` | 10750 W. Grand Avenue, Franklin Park, Ill. 60131       | (312) 451-1360, Chicago 992-2250 |
| `Bally / MIDWAY MFG. CO.`           | 10601 W. Belmont Avenue, Franklin Park, Illinois 60131 | (312) 451-9200                   |

The Grand Avenue sheet also carries `CABLE — MIDCO` and `TELEX NO. 72-1596`. The
Belmont Avenue sheet carries neither and uses the italic Bally script above the
Midway name rather than beside it.

No page examined explains the two addresses or dates the change.

## Scan completeness

The PDFs are page scans without a text layer, both digitised on 24 June 1998 with
GraphicConverter and Acrobat PDFWriter 3.0.1 for Power Macintosh. That is the
scan's date. The bulletins themselves are contemporary with the games.

**notes1 is complete.** The printed page-number footer of all 91 numbered sheets
was read. PDF page 2 is printed page 1, PDF page 92 is printed page 91, and the
run between is unbroken.

**notes2 is missing two pages.** Footers were read for PDF pages 14–86, giving
printed 13–87 with two breaks. PDF pages 2–13 were not swept individually, but
PDF 2 is printed 1 and PDF 13 is printed 12. Twelve sheets for twelve numbers
leaves no room for a gap unless a page is also duplicated. The breaks are:

| Printed page | Contents per the table of contents      | Present in scan |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 31           | `SPACE ENCOUNTER GAME BOARDS`           | no              |
| 37           | `GALAXIAN AND PAC-MAN STATIC KIT` (2/2) | no              |

Neither is a Rally-X page. The gap is easy to miss because the volume's page
count still looks plausible against its table of contents. The offset between PDF
and printed page numbers is `+1` at the start and `−1` by the end, and only the
two breaks in between explain the shift.

## What has been read

The Rally-X bulletin in both volumes, and the cross-game pages named in
[cross-game-pages.md](cross-game-pages.md), were read in full at page resolution.

Both tables of contents were transcribed, and the printed page-number footer of
every numbered sheet in notes1, and of PDF pages 14–86 in notes2, was read to
establish the completeness statement above.

**The remaining 165 pages or so were not read.** They are bulletins for other
games, identified here only by their table-of-contents entries. If something in
this compilation bears on Rally-X without being listed under its name or
appearing on the pages examined, these notes will not have caught it.
